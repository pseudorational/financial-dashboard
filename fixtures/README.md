# Fixture provenance

All fixture data is project-owned, invented data created for this dashboard. No Yahoo captures or third-party financial records are stored here.

`demo_a.json` transcribes the specification's independent Example Manufacturing golden-input table in source units of USD millions. Normalization multiplies by the explicit `source_scale` to obtain base USD. It contains annual FY2021–FY2024 periods with known starts and ends; FY2021 provides opening balances and growth baselines. Publication times are unknown. Net income and equity share parent-stockholder attribution. Cash, investments, and receivables are verified nonoverlapping liquid components for this invented scenario. Borrowing fields exclude finance leases, which are explicitly reported zero.

`DEMO-B` is a deterministic, materially different transformation of those invented inputs: most values ×2.2, costs ×1.8, net income ×3.0, with a separate quote and history. Other scenario symbols deliberately remove a statement or opening history, reduce history to two years, simulate a bank or fund, introduce a loss transition, remove quotes, or simulate an outage.

Daily prices and volume are deterministic mathematical series ending March 31, 2025; they do not represent an actual exchange calendar, security, or return history. Each is labeled synthetic in the interface. The fixed fixture as-of date is April 1, 2025; elapsed wall-clock time does not invent subsequent reports.

Independent rational expected results live in `tests/expected_metrics.json` and are not generated by application calculation code.
